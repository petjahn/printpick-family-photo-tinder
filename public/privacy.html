Privacy Policy for PrintPick

Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Application: PrintPick (Family Photo Culler)

PrintPick (https://petjahn.github.io/printpick-family-photo-tinder/) is a private, family-operated web application designed to help authorized family members filter, organize, and export selected photos from Google Photos for physical photo printing.

1. Information We Access and Collect

  • Google Account Identity: When you sign in with Google via Firebase Authentication, we receive your basic Google profile information (email address and display name) solely to verify that you are an authorized member of the family allowlist.
  • Google Photos Picker Selection (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photospicker.mediaitems.readonly): When you explicitly launch the Google Photos Picker and choose photos, PrintPick receives temporary, read-only metadata and media URLs only for the specific photos you manually selected during that session. PrintPick cannot browse, modify, or delete your entire Google Photos library.

2. How We Use and Store Your Data

  • Photo Status & Compact Thumbnails: When you swipe or categorize a selected photo (as Marked for Print, Marked for Deletion, or Marked as Shown), PrintPick stores the photo's ID, filename, capture timestamp, status, and a compact preview thumbnail in a private Google Cloud Firestore database accessible only to allowlisted family email addresses.
  • Local Browser Cache: Selected photos may be temporarily cached in your local browser's IndexedDB / localStorage so you can download a print-ready .zip archive on your device.
  • No Third-Party Sharing or Selling: We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your Google user data or photos to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers, nor do we use your data for advertising or AI/ML model training.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

PrintPick's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can unmark or remove any photo record at any time directly inside the PrintPick Admin interface, which immediately deletes the record from Cloud Firestore. You may also revoke PrintPick's OAuth access to your Google Account at any time via your Google Account Third-Party Connections page .

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the repository owner via github.com/petjahn/printpick-family-photo-tinder .
